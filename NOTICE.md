| Source | Used for | Terms we follow |
|---|---|---|
| King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex (qurancomplex.gov.sa): Hafs Mushaf text, Tafseer al-Muyassar | Quran text (ground truth), concise tafsir | Text used unmodified and credited; kept separate from generated text |
| QuranEnc.com (Noor International Center): `english_saheeh` | English translation of verses | Credited next to every translation |
| HadeethEnc.com | Hadith text and grades (Arabic + English) | **No modification, addition or deletion of content; HadeethEnc.com is credited in the UI** |

| icadb (icadb.com): Q&A, terminology, dictionary of Quranic words, notable people, places, sects, Names of Allah, books | Scholarly Q&A and reference | Organizers' central database, read through its public API; every card links to its API record |
| Bayyinat (dawa.center/file/7937) | Answers to common doubts | Credited |
| mp3quran.net | Recitation audio links | Links only |
| islamqa.info, binbaz.org.sa, binothaimeen.net | Referral links only | Never copied; a small local list of islamqa question *titles* is used privately for testing |

Hadith grades shown by Baseera come only from HadeethEnc and Dorar, never from the model. Baseera is an AI-powered tool, not a scholar or mufti.
